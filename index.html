Privacy Policy: Device Info & Hardware Test

Effective date: 8 November 2026 Publisher: Techie Bravo Contact: saudpanhwar10@gmail.com

Summary

Device Info & Hardware Test helps you inspect your Android device and test supported hardware. It works locally and doesn't require an account. The app doesn't collect, send, sell or share personal data or device diagnostics. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service.

What the app reads, and what happens to it

WhatWhyWhat happens to it
Device details (model, Android version, processor, memory, storage, display, battery, camera and connectivity capabilities)To show them to youRead while the screen is open, kept in memory, never stored or sent
Sensor readingsFor live readings and sensor checksKept in memory while the screen is open, then discarded. Sensors stop when you leave the screen

Apps on your home screen (only when you open Installed apps)To list them with versions and install datesRead from your phone when the screen opens, shown on screen only, never stored, sent or added to reports. The app can see only apps that appear in your launcher
Wi-Fi and Bluetooth statusTo show whether they're on and what they supportNetwork names, router addresses, Bluetooth device names and nearby devices are never read

The app never accesses your IMEI, serial number, phone number, Android ID, contacts, call logs, messages, photos, files or location. The installed apps list described above stays on your phone.

Diagnostic reports

A report is created only when you ask for one. You see a preview first, then choose where it goes (for example email, messaging or saving to a file) using Android's share sheet or file picker. Techie Bravo never receives a copy. A report contains test results, a basic device summary (model, Android version, chip, memory, storage, display, battery health) and any notes you type. It never contains identifiers, location, network names or recordings. Report files kept so they can be shared are deleted the next time the app starts.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only your appearance preference (light, dark or system) and whether it has already asked for a permission. These stay on your device, are excluded from backups and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you start the feature that needs it, and explains why first. If you decline, only that feature is unavailable. You can change permissions at any time in Android Settings.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any personal data.

Links to Google Play

The About screen has buttons to rate the app, see more apps by Techie Bravo and share the app. These open Google Play or your browser, which have their own privacy policies. The app itself sends nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a feature that uses data is added, we'll update this page and the effective date before the change reaches the app.
